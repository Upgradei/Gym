Guide

Warm-Up · ~8 min

  • 3–5 min easy bike or rower
  • Side-lying thoracic rotations ×8/side · cat-cow ×8
  • Band pull-aparts ×15 · scap push-ups ×10 · band external rotations ×12/side
  • 2–3 ramp-up sets on your first lift

The thoracic + scap work targets your pain pattern and your golf swing. Never skip it.

Pain Rules

  • Sharp or burning → stop the set. Swap to neutral grip, single-arm, chest-supported, or shorten the range.
  • Mild awareness (≤2/10) that isn't worsening → proceed with caution.
  • Ab wheel nervy flare → regress to dead bugs, shorten the range.
  • Hanging hurts? Swap hanging knee raises for lying leg raises.

Daily, even off days: band pull-aparts ×20, doorway pec stretch 30s/side, chin tucks ×10. And book the PT evaluation.

Daily Targets · Lean Bulk

  • Protein: 170–185g — anchor every meal with 40–50g
  • Calories: ~2,750–2,850 (slight surplus)
  • Carbs ~300g, biased around training. Fat ~80g.
  • Creatine 5g · Vitamin D3 2,000 IU
  • Scale: 3–4 mornings/week, weekly average. Target +0.25 to +0.5 lb/wk
  • Gaining faster = mostly fat → trim 150 cal. Not moving in 2 wks → add 150.

Same wake time every day. Build now, reveal in a spring cut.
